# Changelog

## 0.2.0 — voice follow

- Voice follow: press V (or the microphone button) and the script moves as you speak. It waits when you pause, tolerates misheard words, ignores off-script asides, and picks up again after a page jump or a manual scroll. English and Chinese; Chrome, Edge and Safari.
- Voice follow is off until you turn it on, and asks first, saying plainly that the browser's speech service may hear your voice.
- The phone dock fits one more button.

## 0.1.0 — first public release

- Script editor with a page outline, page start times, "Add page" and "Stress" helpers.
- Prompting stage with a reading line, dimmed past lines, stress highlights, page jumps, pace and text-size controls, timer, mirror mode and full screen.
- Pace in words or characters per minute, or worked out from the talk's length.
- Autosave to the browser, Markdown import and export, drag-and-drop import.
- English and Simplified Chinese interface; light and dark themes; phone layout.
- Search-friendly pages: a crawlable guide in English and Chinese, structured data, sitemap and llms.txt.
- Static deployment with no build step; Vercel config with a strict Content Security Policy.
